CECP Musiclib includes a selected, unmodified subset of the rendered acoustic_grand_piano,
acoustic_guitar_steel and electric_bass_finger MP3 notes. Playback transposes nearby
samples and applies gain and note-release envelopes. See sources.json for original
file URLs and SHA-256 hashes. This credit does not imply endorsement by the authors.
